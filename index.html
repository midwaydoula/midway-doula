Hi, I’m Abbie.

Wife, mama of five, and a Traditional Health Worker birth and postpartum doula here in the Rogue Valley. Motherhood shaped who I am, and it’s what led me here — after experiencing the beauty (and intensity) of birth myself, I knew I wanted to walk alongside other women through this sacred season. It’s not just the birth of a baby, but the birth of a mother, too.
