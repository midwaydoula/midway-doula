Hi, I’m Abbie

Wife, mama of five, and your doula in the Rogue Valley.

I’m a Traditional Health Worker, trained birth and postpartum doula serving families throughout the Rogue Valley. Motherhood shaped who I am, and it’s what led me here.

After experiencing the beauty (and intensity) of birth myself — five times over — I knew I wanted to walk alongside other women through this sacred season. It’s not just the birth of a baby, but the birth of a mother, too.

Every woman deserves to feel heard. Every birth deserves evidence-based support. And there’s no one “right” way to do it.

❄

Whether it’s your first baby or your fifth, you don’t have to do this alone.

I’m now booking birth & postpartum clients across the Rogue Valley. I can’t wait to connect with you and get to know your family.
