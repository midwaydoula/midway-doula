Birth Doula Support

Continuous support through labor & delivery

From our first meeting through your birth, I’m your steady, informed presence — helping you understand your options, advocate for your preferences, and feel confident in your body’s ability to bring your baby earthside.

  • Two or more prenatal visits to build your birth preferences
  • On-call support starting at 38 weeks
  • Continuous hands-on comfort measures during labor
  • Partner support & coaching
  • Evidence-based information so you can make informed decisions
  • One postpartum visit to process your birth story

Postpartum Doula Support

Gentle care for the fourth trimester

The birth of a baby is also the birth of a mother. In-home postpartum support gives you space to rest, heal, and bond — while I take care of the rest.

  • Newborn care support & feeding guidance
  • Light meal prep & household support
  • Emotional support & recovery care for mom
  • Sibling support for growing families
  • Flexible daytime & overnight shifts
  • Referrals to trusted local resources as needed
